## How do I invite a teammate?

1. Open **Settings** → **Team**.
2. Enter their name, email, and role. A temporary password is generated for you.
3. Create the account and share that password so they can sign in. They can change it under **Settings** → **Password**.

If PocketBase SMTP is configured, you can also email a setup link. If it is not, the form explains that mail still needs to be turned on in PocketBase admin → **Settings** → **Mail settings**.

## Who can change scan settings?

Admins can change crawl config and schedules. Viewers can usually view findings and start scans, depending on your organization’s roles.

## Tips

- Invite at least one backup admin before handing off day-to-day monitoring.
- Use a shared support email on the invite if multiple people need the welcome message.
