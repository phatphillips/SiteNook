## What appears on the overview?

The site overview summarizes the latest completed scan: accessibility score trends, open issue counts, and shortcuts into Accessibility, QA, and Inventory.

## Where are past scans?

Open **Scans** for the site. Each run shows status (queued, running, complete, failed, or cancelled), page counts, and links into that run’s findings.

## How do pages relate to issues?

Every finding is tied to a **page** inside a **scan**. Filter by URL or open a page to see only issues detected on that URL.
