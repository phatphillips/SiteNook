## How do I add a site?

1. Open **Sites** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Add site** and enter the starting URL (for example `https://example.com`).
3. Save. SiteNook creates the site record and prepares default scan settings.

## How do I run my first scan?

1. Open the site you just added.
2. Click **Scan** (or **Start scan**).
3. Watch live progress while Playwright crawls pages and axe-core checks accessibility.

When the run finishes, open **Accessibility** for issues and scores, or **QA** for broken links and spelling.

## What should I check first?

Start with critical and serious accessibility issues on high-traffic pages. Then review broken links and spelling on the same crawl.
