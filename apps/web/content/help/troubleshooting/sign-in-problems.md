## I keep getting sent to login

Your PocketBase session may have expired. Sign in again. On marketing pages (blog, help, docs) you should stay public even if a stale session expires.

## Wrong organization after login

Use the org switcher if you belong to multiple teams, or clear site data for the app domain and sign in again.

## Password reset

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Check spam for the reset email.
