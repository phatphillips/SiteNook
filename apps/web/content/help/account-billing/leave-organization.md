## How do I leave an organization?

Ask an admin to remove you from the team, or use **Leave organization** if available in your profile. You lose access to that org’s sites and scans.

## How do I close an account?

Contact support or use the account closure flow if enabled. Closing removes access; historical data retention follows your deployment’s policy.

## Tip

Transfer ownership to another admin before the last owner leaves.
