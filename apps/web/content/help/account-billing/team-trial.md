## How do I start a team trial?

From marketing pages, choose **Get started** or **Start team trial** when self-service signup is enabled. Complete account creation and you land in the dashboard with trial limits applied.

## What happens when the trial ends?

You will be prompted to choose a paid plan or the account may lose access to paid features. Export or note critical findings before the trial expires if you are evaluating.

## Who manages billing?

Organization owners typically manage payment methods and invoices in billing settings.
