## Where is billing?

Open **Settings** → **Billing** (wording may vary by plan). From there you can update the card on file and view invoices when Stripe billing is configured.

## Can I change plans?

Yes—upgrade or downgrade from the plan picker. Changes usually apply at the next billing cycle or immediately depending on your Stripe configuration.

## Need a VAT or company name on invoices?

Update the billing profile fields before the next invoice is generated.
