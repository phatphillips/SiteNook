## How do I review an issue?

1. Open **Accessibility** for the site or scan.
2. Select an issue rule to see affected pages and details.
3. Use the explanation and WCAG mapping to plan a fix.

## How do I dismiss a finding?

Use **Dismiss** on an issue when it is a known exception or false positive for your context. Dismissed items stay out of active counts until you restore them.

## Best practice

Document why you dismissed something (ticket link or note) so audits stay explainable.
