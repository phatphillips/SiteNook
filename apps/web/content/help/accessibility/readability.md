## What is readability in SiteNook?

Readability estimates how hard page copy is to read (for example Flesch–Kincaid style scores). It complements axe checks; it is not a WCAG rule by itself.

## When should I act on readability?

Prioritize public content meant for a broad audience—home pages, services, and forms. Dense legal pages may intentionally score harder.

## Tips

- Fix short sentences and clearer headings before rewriting entire articles.
- Compare readability across similar page types, not across the whole site average alone.
