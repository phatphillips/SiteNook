## What does the score mean?

The accessibility score summarizes axe findings for a scan. Higher is better. Trends compare recent completed scans so you can see whether remediations are working.

## Why did my score drop after a deploy?

New templates often introduce contrast, label, or landmark issues. Open the latest scan’s issue list and filter to **new** findings when available.

## How should teams use trends?

Treat the score as a health signal, not a compliance certificate. Pair it with issue counts by severity and page coverage.
