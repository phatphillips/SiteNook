Accessibility is not only about assistive technology. **Clear writing** helps everyone — especially people who are tired, stressed, or reading in a second language.

SiteNook now surfaces readability scores on each crawl so editors can prioritize pages that are harder than they need to be.

## How scoring works

On every scan we compute multiple readability methods (including Flesch–Kincaid grade level). Your site setting chooses which method appears in the dashboard; the scanner still stores the full set for later.

The Readability page shows:

1. Site-average gauge (easy → hard)
2. Distribution buckets across pages
3. A sortable page table

![Readability gauge and distribution](/blog/readability.svg)

## Practical targets

For public information sites, many teams aim for roughly **grade 8–10** on Flesch–Kincaid. Legal and research pages will sit higher — that is expected. Use the distribution to find outliers, not to force every page into the same band.

## Tips for editors

- Prefer shorter sentences and familiar words
- Break long policy pages into sections with clear headings
- Link to plain-language summaries for dense documents

Readability is available on Team and above. Run a scan, then open **Accessibility → Readability** to explore your latest crawl.
